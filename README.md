# Prune

Prune shows what takes up your Android phone's storage, down to every file, and helps you clean it up. It works offline: no internet access, ads or analytics.

**Status:** early version, preparing for release on Google Play.

## Features

- **Storage at a glance:** used and free space, split into Photos, Videos, Audio, Documents, Archives, APKs, Downloads, Apps, System data and Other, which add up to the space in use
- **Down to every file:** browse each category by folder or file type, sort by size, date, name or type, and select many files at once
- **Photos and videos:** grid or list, grouped by the country they were taken in or by size, with a quick full-screen viewer
- **Duplicates:** finds files with identical contents, wherever they are and whatever they're called, and keeps the copy you choose
- **Apps:** every installed app with its size and when you last used it, its data folders, and uninstall
- **System data explained:** what Android itself uses, and what apps keep out of reach
- **Cleanup lists:** large files, old files, temporary files, logs, thumbnail caches, empty folders and leftovers of uninstalled apps
- **Safe deletes:** photos and videos go to the trash, and every delete can be undone right after
- **Always current:** optional background rescans, twice a day and after new photos or downloads
- **One-handed:** titles fill the top of the screen, so lists and buttons sit within thumb's reach
- **Custom look:** light and dark themes, system dynamic colors, and frosted glass on Android 12 or later
- **Essentials mode:** no blur or animations, for speed and battery

## Requirements

Android 10 or later.

## Permissions

Prune asks for each of these on its Access screen, says what it is for, and works without the optional ones.

| Access | Why |
| --- | --- |
| All files access (required) | To see every file on shared storage, not only media, and delete the ones you choose |
| Photos, videos and audio | To read the media library quickly and show thumbnails |
| Photo locations | To group photos by the country they were taken in, worked out on the phone |
| Usage access | To show each app's storage use and when it was last opened |
| Media management | To move photos and videos to the trash without a confirmation for each batch |
| Notifications | To show a scan's progress |
