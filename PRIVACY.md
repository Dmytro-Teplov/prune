# Prune Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 10, 2026

Prune is an Android app that shows what takes up your phone's storage and helps you clean it up. This policy explains how it handles your information.

## Summary

Prune does not collect, sell or share your personal data. It has no internet access, ads or analytics. Everything it reads about your phone stays on your phone.

## Information on your device

To show what uses your storage, Prune reads, on your device only:

- **Files on shared storage:** their names, folders, sizes, types and dates. With All files access, this includes downloads, documents and hidden folders, not only photos, videos and music.
- **File contents, only to find duplicates:** Prune compares files of the same size by computing fingerprints (hashes) of their contents. It doesn't save or share the contents themselves.
- **Photo locations:** if you allow access to photo locations, Prune reads where each photo was taken and works out the country on your device, with a built-in map. It never looks up locations online.
- **Installed apps:** their names, versions, sizes and permissions. With usage access, also their storage use and when you last opened them.
- **Device facts:** storage partitions, memory, battery and processor details shown in System data.

Prune keeps this in a cache on your device so it opens quickly and only rescans what changed. A rescan or Settings can rebuild it at any time. It also stores your settings, such as the theme and sort orders.

## Permissions

Prune asks for each of these on its Access screen and explains what it is for. All are optional except storage access.

- **All files access:** to see and delete every file on shared storage.
- **Photos, videos and audio:** to read your media library.
- **Photo locations:** to group photos by country.
- **Usage access:** to show each app's size and when it was last used.
- **Media management:** to move photos and videos to the trash without a confirmation for each batch.
- **Notifications:** to show a scan's progress.

You can remove any of these at any time in your device's settings.

## Deleting files and apps

Prune only deletes what you choose, after you confirm. Photos and videos go to the system trash, where they can be restored for 30 days. Other files are deleted permanently once the message offering Undo goes away. Uninstalling an app always goes through Android's own confirmation.

## Android backup

If Android backup is turned on, Android may back up Prune's settings to your Google account. The cache of your files, photo locations and apps is never included. Prune has no access to that backup, and Google's privacy policy applies to it.

## Feedback

If you send feedback by email, your mail app sends it, and we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to reply, and don't share it.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling Prune deletes its cache and settings from your device. You can manage backups in your device or Google account settings.

## Children

Prune is not directed at children.
