## Actions you take

Prune shows you what is on your device. Deleting anything is your choice, and Prune always asks you to confirm first:

- **Photos and videos** move to the system trash. You can restore them with Undo, or from your gallery app, until Android removes them after 30 days.
- **Other files** are deleted permanently once the message offering Undo goes away. After that, Prune can't bring them back.
- **Merging duplicates** keeps one copy of each set, the one you pick, and deletes the others the same way.
- **Empty folders** are deleted permanently.
- **Uninstalling an app** opens Android's uninstall screen. If you confirm, Android removes the app and its data.

Before you delete something, make sure you don't need it, or that you have a copy. You are responsible for the actions you choose to take.

## Accuracy
